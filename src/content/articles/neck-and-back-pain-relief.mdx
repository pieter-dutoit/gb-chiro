Neck and back pain are common issues that can affect daily life, work, and overall wellbeing. Whether discomfort comes from posture, injury, stress, or repetitive movement, a clear care plan can help you understand what is happening and what to do next.

## Common Causes

Understanding the possible cause of your pain is the first step toward effective care. Neck and back pain may be linked to:

- Poor posture during work, study, or screen use
- Muscle tension and stress
- Sports or workplace injuries
- Reduced joint mobility
- Repetitive strain or prolonged sitting
- Previous injuries that have changed how you move

1. Assessing how your spine, joints, and muscles are moving
2. Identifying lifestyle or activity factors that may be contributing
3. Providing care aimed at improving movement and easing discomfort
4. Giving practical advice or exercises to support longer-term recovery

## Simple Tips for Relief

Small changes can make a meaningful difference while you are working through pain:

- Take regular movement breaks during long periods of sitting
- Keep screens at eye level where possible
- Use gentle stretching rather than forcing painful movement
- Pay attention to lifting technique and repeated positions
- Seek guidance if pain persists, worsens, or affects your normal routine
